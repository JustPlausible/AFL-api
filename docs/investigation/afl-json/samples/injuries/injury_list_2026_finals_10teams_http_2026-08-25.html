In the mix

Half-back Judd McVee was dropped last week to accommodate a tall backline, but an immediate recall looms if the Dockers want to revert to their usual structure ahead of finals. Ruckman Darcy trained fully after an injury scare in the WAFL and is expected to be available, but young star Reid is unlikely to return this week as he recovers from a knock to the groin area. The Dockers want to play their best available side against Carlton, but if they do choose to manage specific players there is depth at the ready, including midfielder Jeremy Sharp (33 disposals and seven inside 50s in the WAFL) and key defender Hugh Davies (23 and seven marks). O'Meara, Wagner and Walker are also pushing to return after injury layoffs. Young wingman Toby Whan (25 and eight inside 50s) and first-year forward Tobyn Murray (15 and two goals) were emergencies in round 23 and impressed in the WAFL. – Nathan Schmook

Cameron and De Koning must pass fitness tests to face Richmond, with Wiltshire similarly listed as a test following a foot sprain. Meanwhile, Holmes has commenced running, Matofai-Forbes has joined non-contact training ahead of a potential VFL finals return, and Jake Kolodjashnij has been ruled out for AFL selection on Saturday. - Emily Patterson

In the mix

The Hawks are virtually at full strength heading into the last round of the home and away season as they look to recapture some form before the finals. Defender Mraz, who made an impressive start to his AFL career before a kidney injury, is on track to be available for the first week of VFL finals, which coincides with the AFL's wildcard round. Bodie Ryan played a blinder for Box Hill last week with 34 disposals and nine marks, while Finn Maginness (25 disposals, five tackles) and Ned Reeves (13, two goals, 25 hitouts) could also be considered if Sam Mitchell wants to make changes against West Coast. Youngsters Cam Nairn (21 disposals, three goals), Aidan Schubert (15, three), Will McCabe (21, one), Ollie Greeves (26), Calsher Dear (13, one) and Jack Dalton (20, one) all played well in the VFL. - Brandon Cohen

In the mix

The Demons have a decision to make on how best to replace suspended spearhead van Rooyen. Swingman Tom McDonald was reportedly training with the forward group on Tuesday with his switch from defence into attack possibly in the Dees' plans. Veteran forward Jake Melksham has been solid at the lower level, finishing with 3.3 from 15 disposals, seven marks and three tackles on the weekend and could come into the side, while a more like-for-like replacement would be Aidan Johnson (three behinds from 15 disposals, five marks), who has played just the one senior game in 2026. On return from injury, Jack Viney (19 touches, 10 clearances, one goal) and youngster Xavier Lindsay (23 disposals, six marks three clearances) both performed well for Casey. However, coach Steven King hinted that there would be minimal change to the Dees' side ahead of the clash with the Dogs - saying "I am really mindful as well of respecting the group and team at the moment that have got us in this position" - so it appears the duo are unlikely to be recalled. - Alison O'Connor
